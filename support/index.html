Progress

How do I keep my progress safe?

Open the game's Settings (the gear at the top right) and tap Sign in with Game Center. Make sure iCloud Drive is on in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. Once you are signed in, Settings shows when your progress was last backed up.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

  1. On the new phone, sign in to Game Center and iCloud with the same Apple Account.
  2. Install Flame Path and open it.
  3. When the game asks "Saved progress found", choose Load Game Center save.

The game asks "Saved progress found". Which should I pick?

It shows the progress on Game Center next to the progress on this phone. Pick the one you want to keep. The other one is replaced, so read the numbers first.

Settings says "Turn on iCloud Drive to back up your progress."

Your Game Center backup is stored in iCloud Drive. Turn it on in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → iCloud Drive, then reopen the game.

The sign-in button opens iOS Settings instead of signing me in.

That happens after the Game Center sign-in has been dismissed. Sign in under Game Center in iOS Settings, then come back to the game.

I deleted the game without signing in. Can you restore my progress?

Unfortunately not. Without Game Center, progress lives only on the phone, and we do not keep a copy of it.

Purchases

I bought something and did not get it.

Close the game completely and open it again; a purchase that was interrupted is delivered on the next launch. If the purchase needed approval (Ask to Buy), it arrives once it is approved. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date and the item.

How do I get Remove Ads or the Torch back on a new phone?

Go to Shop → Offers and tap Restore purchase. Gems are used up as you spend them, so they cannot be restored this way, but they are part of your progress and come back with your Game Center backup.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

How do I turn ads off?

Buy Remove Ads in the shop, or any gem pack. Either one switches off the banner and the between-run ads for good. Ads you choose to watch for coins stay available.

How do I change my ad consent?

In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, open the game's Settings and tap Privacy options. Tracking permission can be changed anywhere in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I see or delete my data?

Email us and we will help. What we keep, and why, is in the privacy policy.
