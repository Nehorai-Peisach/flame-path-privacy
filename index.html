Flame Path Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · Applies to the iOS app com.flamepath.flamepath

The short version. Your progress is saved on your device. If you sign in with Game Center, a backup copy is kept in your own iCloud Drive, where only you can reach it.

To keep purchases honest and the game fair, our server keeps a small record for each Game Center player: a random player ID, the App Store receipts of your purchases, and a daily summary of your progress. We never collect your name, email address or Apple Account.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google collects data to serve them. The sections below say exactly what goes where and how to limit it.

1. Who we are

Flame Path is developed and published by Nehorai Peisach ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game.

2. What the game stores on your device

The game saves its data locally, in the app's own storage, using Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism. It consists of:

  • Your progress: levels cleared, towers unlocked, ranks and cards.
  • Your in-game currency balances and inventory.
  • Your settings, such as sound and haptics.
  • Counters used to ration ads: how many you have seen today, and when the last one was shown.
  • Whether you have made a purchase that turns ads off.
  • A random token, created on your device, that your purchases are made under so they can be matched to your save.

Deleting the app deletes this copy. If you have signed in with Game Center, the backup described below lets you get your progress back.

3. Game Center and your backup

Signing in with Game Center is optional, and is offered in the game's Settings. When you are signed in:

  • A copy of your save is kept in your own iCloud Drive, through Apple's Game Center saved games. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, it counts toward your iCloud storage, and we cannot see or change it.
  • If the copy in iCloud and the one on your device ever differ, for example on a new phone, the game shows you both and lets you choose which to keep. Nothing is replaced without asking you.

Game Center itself is run by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of it in iOS Settings at any time.

4. What our server keeps

When you are signed in with Game Center, the game sends a small record to our server. This is the only personal data we hold:

WhatWhen
Game Center player ID. A random identifier Apple assigns, which only works within our games. It is not your name, nickname or Apple Account.With every record
Purchase receipts. The receipt Apple signs for each purchase: what was bought, when, its transaction ID, and the random token from section 2.After each purchase
Progress summary. Your level, experience, coins, gems, stars, levels cleared, the app version, and whether the save appears to have been edited outside the game.Once a day

Why. To confirm that purchases are real and to detect cheating, so that the game can stay fair if it ever adds features where players meet each other. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the hosting provider below.

Where. The server is hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany), which processes it on our behalf.

How long. For as long as the game is offered, or until you ask us to delete it (section 9).

Legal basis (for players in the EEA and the UK): our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and keeping the game fair, and, for purchase receipts, providing what you bought.

If you do not sign in with Game Center, nothing in this section is sent.

Game content

The game's pictures, music and level data are downloaded from the same provider when the game needs them. As with any download, the server receives your device's IP address in order to send the files. We do not use it to identify you, and it is not stored with any of the records above.

5. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, in three forms: a small banner while a level is being played, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins.

To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device. According to Google's own published privacy manifest for the SDK, that is:

"Coarse location" here means an approximate area derived from your network address, roughly city level. The game does not request access to your device's location services, and will never ask you for precise location.

How Google handles this data is governed by Google's own policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob and third-party ad providers

6. Your choices about ads

Tracking permission (all regions)

On iOS, the game asks once whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalised ads instead. Nothing else about the game changes. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent (Europe, the UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form the first time you play, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You may refuse, and you may change your decision later from Settings → Privacy options inside the game.

Turning ads off

Ads that you did not ask for, the banner and the between-run ad, are switched off permanently if you buy the "Remove Ads" item, or if you buy any gem pack. Ads you choose to watch in exchange for coins remain available, because you are never shown one without pressing a button first.

7. Purchases

All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see and never receive your payment details, card number, billing address or Apple Account information. What the game receives from Apple is a signed receipt naming the item bought and its transaction ID. The game uses it to credit your gems or switch ads off, and keeps it as described in section 4. Apple's handling of the transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Children

Flame Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent, under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA.

To see or delete the records our server keeps about you (section 4), email us. We will reply within 30 days. Because we do not know your name or email address, we will ask for details that let us find your records, such as the dates and items of your purchases.

Requests about data collected for advertising should be directed to Google, which acts as the controller for that data. You can also always: withdraw consent from Settings → Privacy options, revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings, sign out of Game Center, or delete the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's App Store listing.
