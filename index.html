Flame Path — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · Applies to the iOS app com.flamepath.flamepath

The short version. Flame Path has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved on your device and nowhere else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

The game does show ads, and those are served by Google AdMob. Google — not us — collects data in order to serve them. That is the only place your information goes, and the sections below say exactly what it is and how to limit it.

1. Who we are

Flame Path is developed and published by Nehorai Peisach ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game.

2. What the game itself stores

Everything the game saves is stored locally on your device, in the app's own storage, using Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism. It is not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot see it. It consists of:

  • Your progress — levels cleared, towers unlocked, ranks and cards.
  • Your in-game currency balances and inventory.
  • Your settings, such as sound and haptics.
  • Counters used to ration ads — how many you have seen today, and when the last one was shown.
  • Whether you have made a purchase that turns ads off.

None of this identifies you. There is no name, no email address, no account and no profile. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently; because it is never backed up to a server of ours, we cannot restore it for you.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, in three forms: a small banner while a level is being played, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins.

To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device. According to Google's own published privacy manifest for the SDK, that is:

WhatUsed forLinked to you

"Coarse location" here means an approximate area derived from your network address — roughly city level. The game does not request access to your device's location services, and will never ask you for precise location.

How Google handles this data is governed by Google's own policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob and third-party ad providers

4. Your choices about ads

Tracking permission (all regions)

On iOS, the game asks once whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalised ads instead. Nothing else about the game changes. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent (Europe, the UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form the first time you play, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You may refuse, and you may change your decision later from Settings → Privacy options inside the game.

Turning ads off

Ads that you did not ask for — the banner and the between-run ad — are switched off permanently if you buy the "Remove Ads" item, or if you buy any gem pack. Ads you choose to watch in exchange for coins remain available, because you are never shown one without pressing a button first.

5. Purchases

All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see and never receive your payment details, card number, billing address or Apple Account information. What the game receives from Apple is a transaction identifier and the name of the item bought, which is what tells it to credit your gems or to switch ads off. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

Flame Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will act on it.

7. Data retention

We hold nothing to retain — no data ever reaches us. Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own retention schedules, described in the policies linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent, under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give you or delete on our side. Requests about data collected for advertising should be directed to Google, who acts as the controller for that data. You can also always: withdraw consent from Settings → Privacy options, revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings, or delete the app.

You are welcome to contact us about any of this, and we will help where we can.
